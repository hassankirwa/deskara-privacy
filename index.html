Deskara privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Applies to the Deskara app for Android (package app.deskara).

In short

Deskara collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it cannot connect to the internet: the app does not request Android's internet permission, so the system blocks it from the network.

Your files

Deskara opens only the files and folders you choose, through Android's own file picker or when you open a file with Deskara from another app. Your documents are processed entirely on your phone. Temporary working copies, recovery checkpoints and thumbnails are kept in the app's private storage on your phone and are deleted when no longer needed, or when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Deskara never sends your files anywhere. A file leaves your phone only when you share it yourself, using Android's share sheet, to an app you pick.

Camera

Deskara asks for the camera only when you use Scan. Pictures are processed on your phone and saved only where you choose. You can refuse or withdraw the camera permission at any time in Android's settings; everything else keeps working.

Fonts and language packs

Fonts and text-recognition language packs that you import are copied into the app's private storage so they can be used offline. They stay on your phone.

Settings

Your preferences (such as theme, language, recent files and paper size) are stored on your phone only. Deskara opts out of Android's cloud backup, so they are not copied anywhere.

Children

Deskara collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. A version of Deskara that collected data would need a new policy before release, and this page would say so plainly.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@deskara.app. Deskara is open source; you can check these statements in the source code.
